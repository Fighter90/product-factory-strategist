The run’s final report: the state of the market, segments and personas, red-teamed options, tactics, the beta plan and decision thresholds. Assembled with the pfs export --six-pager command; every number traces back to a registry claim.

Calculations

Four workbooks with live formulas

The workbooks live in outputs/attachments/: editable inputs on the input sheet — formulas recalculate on open, assumptions change on the fly. Every input value traces back to a registry claim.

unit_economics.xlsx

Per-paying-user contribution $21,75, LTV ≈ $272, LTV/CAC ≈ 4,5, sensitivity to price and churn.

Open workbook

growth_model.xlsx

A 12-month growth model: the funnel visits → signups → activations → paying users → MRR, cross-checked against the beta expectation.

Open workbook

cohort_retention.xlsx

Weekly retention cohorts: return rates of activated and paying users — the base for the validation plan.

Open workbook

market_sizing.xlsx

The addressable online-recruiting market: $37.97B as the base, the conservative floor of the estimate range, and the path to SOM through conversion rates.
