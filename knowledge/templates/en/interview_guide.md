| Block | Questions (about past experience, no leading hints) | What we look for |
|---|---|---|
| Warm-up (3 min) | Tell us how you usually … | context |
| The last occasion (10 min) | When was the last time you …? What happened right before? | trigger, situation |
| Journey and alternatives (10 min) | How did you solve it? What else did you try? Why did you settle on …? | alternatives, criteria |
| Barriers (10 min) | What got in the way / was annoying? What almost made you give up? | barriers |
| Value and price (5 min) | What does this cost you today (money, time)? | value |
| Wrap-up (2 min) | What didn't I ask that I should have? | blind spots |
