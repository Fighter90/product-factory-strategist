# Continuous Discovery (EN summary)

**When:** the team ships in sprints and cannot afford month-long discovery projects; you need a continuous stream of user contact and a transparent "risk → opportunity → solution" map.

**Core:** Opportunity Solution Tree (product goal → opportunities phrased as user needs → solutions → experiments). Weekly touchpoints: someone on the team talks to a user every week (20–30 min interview snippets). Three parallel risks are always assessed: value, usability, feasibility/business. Interview snippets accumulate as structured facts. Solution mini-canvas: biggest risk → most dangerous assumption → smallest test.

**How:** quarterly product goal → tree from interviews → touchpoint cadence → for the top opportunity: 3+ alternative solutions and an assumption map → test the most dangerous assumption first.

**Pitfalls:** a tree of features instead of opportunities; touchpoints "when there is time"; testing without targeting the riskiest assumption.
