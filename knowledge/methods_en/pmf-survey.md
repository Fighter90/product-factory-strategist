# PMF Survey (EN summary)

**When:** the product has been live for a few months with active users; you need to know whether PMF exists and on which segment before investing in growth.

**Core:** ask active users "How would you feel if you could no longer use the product?" — benchmark: **≥40% "very disappointed"** signals PMF potential. The value is in segmentation: profile who those 40% are (role, scenario, frequency, channel), narrow positioning to them (Superhuman method), and use open answers ("What is the main benefit?") as positioning copy. A high survey score without retention is a false signal.

**How:** survey in-product for actives only → 40–80+ responses → score + cuts → pick the best-scoring segment → interviews → value proposition in their words → decide: grow or go back to discovery.

**Pitfalls:** surveying everyone including one-time users; trusting the average without cuts; measuring once and forever.
