# Dual-Track Agile (EN summary)

**When:** the team ships in sprints but discovery happens "whenever", and sprints are built from unvalidated features.

**Core:** Discovery track answers "what to build" (interviews, prototypes, value tests); Delivery track turns validated ideas into working product. Tracks run in parallel inside ONE team. Definition of ready = value, usability, feasibility and business risk validated. Part of every sprint's capacity is discovery work (20–30% typical). Artifacts are short-lived and updated: opportunity tree, assumption map, prototypes, experiments with pre-set criteria.

**How:** reserve discovery capacity per sprint → idea pipeline (tree → assumptions → tests → ready) → retro on both tracks ("what we shipped" AND "what we learned").

**Pitfalls:** discovery without delivery = endless research; delivery without discovery = a conveyor of unvalidated features; separate teams for the tracks.
