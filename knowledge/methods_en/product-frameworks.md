# Product Frameworks: What, When, Why (EN summary)

**When:** choosing a tool for the current phase and question. Agent rule: a framework is a checklist and a vocabulary, not "the right answer"; every framework must be filled with data from the C###/S### claim registry and calculations, otherwise it is useless.

**Core:** phase → framework map (double diamond): **Discover** (what's happening? who is the user? → JTBD, audience research, PESTEL, trends/scenarios, Double Diamond itself); **Define** (which problem do we solve? what's true? → problem design, competitors, the situation framework, Lean Canvas); **Develop** (how do we solve it? what's the priority? → drivers and bets, RICE/ICE/WSJF, Kano, cannibalization, platforms); **Deliver** (how do we verify and record it? → A/B testing, NSM/OKR, product metrics, six-pager, DACI). Prioritization quick choice: **RICE** = Reach × Impact × Confidence / Effort — when you have reach numbers and confidence estimates; for a quarterly backlog. **ICE** = Impact × Confidence × Ease — a quick sprint-horizon estimate; less data, more arguing. **WSJF** = weight/time — when cost of delay differs across initiatives (seasonality, regulation). The discipline is identical: criteria fixed **before** scoring; Confidence must cite C### claims. Metric families: **AARRR** — the growth funnel (acquisition → activation → retention → revenue → referral): find the bottleneck; **HEART** — product quality: Happiness, Engagement, Adoption, Retention, Task success; **Kano** — which features are basic / performance / delight, for surveys and roadmap; **NSM + metrics tree** — one North Star and its drivers, the input for OKR. Hypothesis-check cycles: **HADI** (Hypothesis → Action → Data → Insight) — the micro-loop of every bet: a hypothesis from a C### claim, an action, data, a conclusion → update (supersede) the claim — and repeat; **MVP/pilot** — the verification type matched to the risk; **backtest** — for models and forecasts.

**How:** (1) identify the double-diamond phase and the question — pick 1–2 frameworks from the map, no more; (2) fill the framework from the claim registry: every cell/score gets a C###/S### reference or an explicit "EXAMPLE" mark; (3) derive a testable consequence from the framework → add it to the validation plan or record it as a forecast claim.

**Pitfalls:** framework for framework's sake — filling everything in sequence instead of the one needed; scores without confidence (RICE with Confidence=100% on unverified data); fitting the result — changing prioritization criteria after seeing the scores; collecting — 6 frameworks on one question yield zero new knowledge after the third.
