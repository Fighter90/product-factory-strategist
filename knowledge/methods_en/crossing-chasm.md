# Crossing the Chasm (EN summary)

**When:** early enthusiasts love the product but growth stalls — "almost the same" segments do not convert. Classic chasm between early adopters and early pragmatics.

**Core:** pragmatics buy whole solutions for their industry with references from "people like them". Fix: pick ONE beachhead niche (segment + scenario + problem) where the product becomes the whole solution — and dominate it. Whole product = everything a pragmatic needs (support, integrations, docs, references), discovered via interviews, not feature lists. Bowling alley: expand to adjacent niches only after dominance. Positioning per Moore: "for [segment] who [need], [product] is … unlike [alternative]".

**How:** segment discovery → beachhead choice (pain, willingness to pay, access, no dominant whole-competitor) → whole-product map → positioning → references → next niche.

**Pitfalls:** "our product is for everyone"; growing into non-native segments before dominance; whole product assembled from a feature list.
